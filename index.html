How to work a deal:
Before you start: Open the deal's full detail page (click into it, don't just skim the card) and read the whole thing — that's where the Open Drive Folder link lives, with photos and the full deal overview to actually show an interested buyer. Missing that link means pitching blind and having nothing to send when a buyer asks to see it.
Step 1. Call buyers from our lead list — this is the main way to work a deal. Match yourself to buyers on the deal below (or cold-call specific cities from your own list) and start dialing. Cold emailing buyers is also allowed — but talk to admin first before you send any, since there's a specific outreach approach we use to keep those emails out of spam and avoid burning our sending reputation.
Step 2. As soon as a buyer shows real interest, log them under "Interested Buyers" on that deal — this emails admin right away. Keep the conversation notes updated there as things progress, and mark the match Negotiating, Closing, or Dead as it plays out, so it's easy to follow which deals are closing. Follow up with everyone who's asked for more info every 1–3 days — mark that in their notes each time so it's clear when they were last touched. A deal's info banner also has a Copy Info To Send Buyer button once you're ready to text or email someone the details — the moment anyone you send that to says they're interested, contact admin right away so we can get them the address; don't wait, and don't send it yourself.
Facebook posting — optional, and not the main way we work deals right now. Calling buyers directly gets far more control than a public post, so please lead with Step 1 above instead. If you do want to try a Facebook post, it needs admin approval first (see the form further down this deal's page) — once a post is approved and shared to a bunch of groups, there's no way to pull it back or delete it if something's wrong with it (wrong price, deal falls through, a typo), so admin reviews these carefully and this stays a secondary option for now.
Address disclosure. Every deal's exact street address is locked by default — you'll only ever see its Deal Code, City/State/Zip, County, price, and other general info until admin specifically grants you that one deal's address. Pitch buyers off that general info first. Only once a buyer has actually responded to that pitch, is genuinely interested, and specifically asks you for the address — never before, and never just because a deal looks like a good fit to you — hit Request Address Access on that deal to ask admin for it. Never share an address you haven't been granted, and never share a granted address with anyone besides that one legitimate, matched buyer who asked for it. Sharing it early, or with the wrong person, risks losing us the deal entirely and getting removed as a team member.
Got an interested buyer? Text to let us know, in addition to logging them under Interested Buyers above. Send:
"Interested and want more information?"

How to work a buyer lead:

Everything below was given to you specifically for a deal we currently have to sell — you won't see buyers with nothing active to offer them.

Step 1. Reach out about the deal shown on the card — always call first, never text as your opening move. High-volume texting to numbers with no reply history is what gets a phone number blocked from texting, so calls are necessary in the beginning. Texting unlocks for a buyer only after they've responded to a call. Try to reach out between 8am and 7pm in the buyer's own time zone for a first outreach — each buyer's card shows their current local time. It's a guideline, not a lockout: if a buyer calls or texts you outside that window, or you're just working a little earlier or later, go ahead and log it.
Step 2. No response within 24 hours? Call again. Once they respond, you can also follow up with a text if it's useful. That's the full SOP — once both touches are logged, the pitch is marked Fully Worked.
Not the right fit? Update their General Notes (price range, ARV%, areas of interest) so we can match them correctly the next time a deal comes up — even in a different city.
How to Build Your Own Buyer List (click to expand)

A step-by-step guide, not an automated integration — Propwire and skip-trace steps below all happen on those sites directly; come back here to import the CSV once you have it.

Step 1 — Find cash buyers on Propwire

Go to propwire.com and start a new search. Filter to Cash Buyers, then pick the category that matches the deal you're building a list for:

  • Fix & Flip deals — filter to buyers with a recent Fix & Flip purchase.
  • Land deals — switch Property Type to Land instead, still Cash Buyers.

Then set Portfolio Value to a range of roughly 3x to 20x the subject property's price — e.g. a $100,000 deal → filter buyers with a $300,000–$2,000,000 portfolio. Aim for a master list of 500+ buyers if the filters return that many — more to work with over time.

Step 2 — Split it into batches of 100

Recommended: work 100 buyers at a time per deal, not the whole master list at once. Copy the first 100 rows of your export into a new CSV, then the next 100 into another, and so on until your 500+ list is split into 5 (or more) separate CSVs of 100 each. Skip trace and import them one batch at a time as you actually work through them, rather than all at once.

Step 3 — Skip trace each batch of 100

  • Manually, for free, one at a time at truepeoplesearch.com.
  • Have a VA do the manual lookups for you at scale.
  • Pay Propwire's own built-in skip trace on the export.
  • Pay a third-party skip-trace vendor to run the whole list at once.

Do this quickly — don't spend all day skip tracing. Skip tracing doesn't make money by itself; it just gets you phone numbers. The only action that actually moves the needle is calling buyers, getting a deal in front of them, and asking if they're interested. Skip trace one batch of 100, then get on the phone.

Step 4 — Import it below

Once you have a CSV with names and phone numbers, upload it below. This list is private to you — nobody else on the team can see it or be given these leads, only you.

Working the list: call/voicemail first, never cold text. These are skip-traced numbers with no prior relationship or reply history — texting them cold is exactly what gets a phone number flagged and blocked from texting by carriers. Always call first, and leave a voicemail (there's a checkbox for it when you log the call) if there's no answer. Texting only opens up after that person has actually responded to a call.

Volume target: reach out to 50+ buyers a day. That's the pace that actually gets a deal sold — a smaller number of calls a day just means a deal sits longer.

Give New Reps Access To The Open Pool

One click instead of hand-assigning a new signup to every open deal individually. Sweeps every active, unlocked deal's "All Users With No Assigned Deals Right Now" — same rule as doing it deal by deal: a rep only stays out of it if they've been hand-picked (Add Access/Assign Myself) for something specifically, or given the Bulk Assign Override toggle. A deal you've Locked to a specific rep's target market is skipped entirely, and it's safe to click this again later (already-swept reps aren't re-added or duplicated) — so it's fine to just run this whenever someone new signs up.

When on, this same open-pool sweep runs automatically the instant someone signs up — they see active deals right away with no admin action needed, instead of waiting on you to notice the signup notification (which can get missed, e.g. around a backend update) and run the button above by hand. Same rules apply either way — Locked deals stay reserved, and a rep hand-picked for something specific elsewhere still doesn't get swept into everything.
