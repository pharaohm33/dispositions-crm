How to work a deal:
Step 1. Contact buyers from our lead list, or send us your Facebook post for approval before posting it (including which groups you intend to post it to) using the form on a deal below. Cold emailing buyers is also allowed — but talk to admin first before you send any, since there's a specific outreach approach we use to keep those emails out of spam and avoid burning our sending reputation.
Step 2. As soon as a buyer shows real interest, log them under "Interested Buyers" on that deal — this emails admin right away. Keep the conversation notes updated there as things progress, and mark the match Negotiating, Closing, or Dead as it plays out, so it's easy to follow which deals are closing.

How to work a buyer lead:

Everything below was given to you specifically for a deal we currently have to sell — you won't see buyers with nothing active to offer them.

Step 1. Reach out about the deal shown on the card — always call first, never text as your opening move. High-volume texting to numbers with no reply history is what gets a phone number blocked from texting, so calls are necessary in the beginning. Texting unlocks for a buyer only after they've responded to a call. Try to reach out between 8am and 7pm in the buyer's own time zone for a first outreach — each buyer's card shows their current local time. It's a guideline, not a lockout: if a buyer calls or texts you outside that window, or you're just working a little earlier or later, go ahead and log it.
Step 2. No response within 24 hours? Call again. Once they respond, you can also follow up with a text if it's useful. That's the full SOP — once both touches are logged, the pitch is marked Fully Worked.
Not the right fit? Update their General Notes (price range, ARV%, areas of interest) so we can match them correctly the next time a deal comes up — even in a different city.

Give Buyer Leads for a Deal

Gives a batch of buyer leads to one team member, paired specifically with the deal you pick below — that pairing (a "pitch") is what shows up in their queue. Leads already pitched for this same deal are skipped.

Leave city/state/zip blank to match the deal's own location automatically.

Auto-Feed

When on, checks every active deal (not Sold/Dead): any team member with access to it who's worked through everything they've been given for it is automatically given more matching buyer leads for that same deal. A team member's Preferred Buyer-Lead Area (Team tab), if set, limits which of their deals qualify. Runs whenever you click below, or hourly if you've installed the optional trigger — see SETUP.md.
