Why Lockhart

Lockhart is the Caldwell County seat, roughly thirty miles southeast of downtown Austin on the US 183 corridor, and is known nationally as the Barbecue Capital of Texas.

The investment case is proximity. Austin's housing costs have pushed working households outward for years, and the towns along 183 and SH 130 have absorbed that demand. Lockhart has a historic courthouse square, a real small-town identity, and commuting distance to both Austin and San Marcos, with the SH 130 industrial corridor in between.

The rental number tells the same story: estimated market rent on this property is $3,137 a month against a $195,000 acquisition — a ratio not found inside Travis County.
