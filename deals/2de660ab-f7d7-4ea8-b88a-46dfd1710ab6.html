Why New Braunfels

New Braunfels sits in Comal County on Interstate 35, roughly halfway between San Antonio and Austin, and it has been one of the consistently fastest growing cities in Texas for more than a decade.

The demand drivers are durable. It is a genuine bedroom community for both metros, it carries real tourism from the Comal and Guadalupe rivers, Schlitterbahn, and the Gruene historic district, and it has an employment base of its own rather than depending entirely on commuters.

A four bedroom, three and a half bath house with 2,513 square feet is family product in a market that absorbs family product.
