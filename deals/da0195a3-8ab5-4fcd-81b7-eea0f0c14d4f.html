The Location — This Is the Whole Argument

Haymount is Fayetteville's premier residential address, and this parcel sits on its edge.

County parcel data shows the properties adjoining and immediately behind this lot assessed at approximately $1.3 million, $1.4 million, $1.5 million, $1.5 million, and $1.9 million. That is the Hybarts Branch and Willow Bend Lane estate section, and the rear property line of this parcel touches it.

Be clear-eyed about what that does and does not mean. It does not mean this house becomes a million-dollar house — it is 1,169 square feet of 1942 frame construction, and it will be worth what renovated 1,169-square-foot houses in this pocket are worth.

What it does mean is that the land under it sits in a location that does not exist elsewhere at this price, that the neighborhood behind it is not going anywhere, and that a finished product here competes at the top of its size class rather than the middle.

One more detail worth attention: the market analysis notes that several of the closest as-is comparable sales sit across Bragg Boulevard. In Fayetteville, Bragg Boulevard is a real dividing line. Those comps match the subject on size and layout, but they are not on the Haymount side. The subject is.

That is a meaningful reason the $124-per-square-foot as-is figure may understate this specific lot, and a meaningful reason the $215-per-square-foot after-repair figure is reachable rather than optimistic. Verify independently against renovated Haymount-side closings.

The Two Valuation Figures — and What Each One Means

Two numbers exist on this property. They answer different questions and are not interchangeable.

The $251,029 figure is the after-repair value, drawn from renovated comparable sales. That is the exit.

The $149,841 figure is an estimated as-is value, drawn from a separate set of non-renovated comparables. Here is the caution worth giving now rather than discovering later: the homes in that as-is set were dated but functional, not full-gut condition. One of them was described in its own listing as having modern updates throughout, updated bath fixtures, and new flooring. The subject here is in materially rougher shape than that.

Do not treat $149,841 as a floor. Underwriting should run off the $251,029 exit and your own renovation scope.
