What We Have

This is an approximately 1.09-acre / 47,480-square-foot residential development parcel located on Grinnell Street in the Town of Lewisville, North Carolina. The property is not located within a subdivision and is currently zoned RS-9 — Residential Single-Family.

The primary upside is the potential to divide the existing parcel into two residential homesites. Lewisville's RS-9 zoning generally requires a minimum zoning lot area of 9,000 square feet and a minimum lot width of 65 feet. At approximately 47,480 square feet, the subject contains substantially more gross land area than would be required for two minimum-size RS-9 lots.

The property is being sold as one parcel. No representation is being made that two separate legal or independently buildable parcels currently exist. Buyer must independently confirm frontage, legal access, subdivision approval, floodplain conditions, utility connections, setbacks, drainage, and the final configuration of any proposed two-lot split.

Public water and sewer serve the immediate Grinnell Street area, creating an important development advantage over sites requiring individual wells and septic systems.

The subject is being offered at $34,900. If a two-lot configuration is ultimately approved, that represents an illustrative raw-land basis of $29,950 per potential homesite. This calculation is provided solely as an underwriting illustration — the property has not been represented as two existing legal lots.
