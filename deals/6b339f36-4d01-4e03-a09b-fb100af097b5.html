This is an opportunity to acquire approximately 1.90 gross acres across two adjoining tax parcels along the S 6th Avenue corridor in Tucson, Arizona. Why Buyers Should Look at This

The primary opportunity is the ability to evaluate approximately 1.90 gross acres across two adjoining parcels as one infill development site.

  • The lower parcel provides the larger and broader portion of the site, while the adjoining upper parcel adds additional acreage and frontage that may improve overall site-planning flexibility.
  • The R-2 component creates a residential development angle for local infill builders and small developers. Tucson permits middle-housing uses within R-2, making duplex or small middle-housing concepts worth investigating.
  • A potential 3-4 dwelling concept may be worth evaluating with the City, but no specific unit count, subdivision layout, or development approval is being represented.

Transit & Development Angle

  • Both parcels screened within the mapped transit buffer associated with Route 27 Midvale / Valencia, with transit access immediately along the S 6th Avenue corridor.
  • Qualifying middle-housing projects within applicable transit areas may be eligible for parking-related relief, subject to project-specific City confirmation. No parking waiver is being represented as automatically available.
  • The combination of adjoining parcels, approximately 1.90 gross acres, paved frontage, nearby transit, and a residential zoning component creates a site worth evaluating for small-scale infill development.
