Overview

This is an off-market opportunity to acquire Phase One of a planned residential community sitting on the US-501 corridor near Aynor and Conway, in Horry County, South Carolina. Phase One comprises 88 residential lots across a 101.19-acre tract, and the full master-planned tract (the broader "Hucks" reference plan) is designed for 170 lots total, meaning this offering represents a majority-share entry point into a larger community.

The supplied master concept plan calls for a minimum lot size of 10,000 square feet and a minimum lot width of 55 feet. Public water and sewer service, along with public roads, are both noted on the master plan. Meaningful due diligence has already been completed on this file: a Phase I Environmental Site Assessment (ESA) has been performed, and roadway geotechnical diligence has also been completed, along with rezoning documentation on file for the tract.

The site sits along the same corridor as Horry County's newly approved 500-acre Rural Civic Center project, which is moving toward construction and is expected to bring additional growth momentum to the surrounding Aynor/Conway area.

Buyer is responsible for independently verifying final Phase One lot yield, boundary lines, entitlement status, and development costs prior to closing.

Additional photos, a general location map, and deal-overview materials for this tract are available upon request.
