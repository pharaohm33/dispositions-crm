Overview

Off-market land opportunity in Horry County, South Carolina: 88 residential lots representing Phase One of a larger 170-lot planned community on the Hucks Tract, positioned directly on the US-501 corridor near Aynor and Conway, SC. The total assembled site spans approximately 101.19 acres.

Diligence work has already been completed on the file — this is not a blind land purchase. A completed Phase I Environmental Site Assessment and roadway geotechnical report are both on hand, along with a rezoning submission for the tract.

The parcel sits along the same US-501 corridor as Horry County's newly approved 500-acre Rural Civic Center project, which is moving toward construction and is expected to bring additional growth momentum to the surrounding area.

This listing covers land only — there are no existing residential improvements. Beds, baths, half-baths, square footage, year built, and parking are not applicable to this offering.
