Deal Overview

"Got an off-market 88-lot deal in Horry County, SC — diligence already done." This is Phase One of a larger 170-lot planned community in Horry County, South Carolina, positioned directly on the US-501 corridor near Aynor and Conway. The site sits along the same corridor as Horry County's newly approved 500-acre Rural Civic Center project, which is moving toward construction and is expected to bring additional growth momentum to the area.

The offering covers Phase One only of the broader Hucks Tract reference plan. A completed Phase I Environmental Site Assessment and roadway geotechnical diligence are already in the deal file, meaning this is not a blind land acquisition — real due diligence has already been performed and is available for buyer review.
