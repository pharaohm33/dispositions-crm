Overview

Off-market opportunity to acquire Phase One of the Hucks Tract, an 88-lot residential land position in Horry County, South Carolina, sitting directly on the US-501 corridor near Aynor and Conway. Phase One is the initial release of a larger master-planned community with a broader reference plan of 170 total lots across the full tract.

Diligence on the file is further along than a typical raw land buy: a Phase I Environmental Site Assessment has been completed, and roadway geotechnical diligence has also been completed for the planned infrastructure. The supplied master concept plan calls for a minimum lot size of 10,000 square feet and a minimum lot width of 55 feet, with public water, public sewer, and public roads all noted on the master plan.

The site also benefits from proximity to Horry County's newly approved 500-acre Rural Civic Center project, which is moving toward construction along the same corridor and is expected to support continued growth momentum in the surrounding area.

Buyer is responsible for independently verifying final Phase One lot yield, boundary lines, entitlement status, and development costs prior to closing.
