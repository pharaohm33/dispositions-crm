Overview

An off-market opportunity to control Phase One of a larger planned residential community in Horry County, South Carolina — 88 residential lots on 101.19 acres, positioned along the US-501 corridor near Aynor and Conway. The broader Hucks Tract reference plan calls for 170 lots total, with this offering covering Phase One only. Diligence work has already been completed on the site, including a Phase I Environmental Site Assessment and roadway geotechnical study, so this is not a blind land purchase — there is a real diligence file behind it.

The corridor is also benefiting from broader momentum: Horry County's newly approved 500-acre Rural Civic Center project sits along the same corridor and is moving toward construction, which should support continued growth in the surrounding area.

This offering is an assignment of contract. JNA Dynamic Holdings LLC holds an equitable interest under contract with the underlying property owner and is marketing that contract position for assignment — this is not a direct sale by the property owner, and this page does not constitute an offer of direct contact with the seller.
