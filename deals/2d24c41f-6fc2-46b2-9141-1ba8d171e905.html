Deal Overview

This is an 88-lot Phase One residential development opportunity in Horry County, South Carolina, positioned within the larger Hucks Tract development concept along the U.S. Highway 501 corridor. The current offering is specifically limited to the 88 Phase One lots.

The broader Hucks Tract planning package contains a documented residential civil concept, public infrastructure notes, Phase I environmental diligence, and roadway-focused geotechnical work already completed. The supplied master planning exhibit shows a broader 170-lot residential concept, with 10,000-square-foot minimum lots, 55-foot minimum lot widths, public roads, public water and sewer notes, open space, sidewalks, pocket parks, and a layout designed around the site's wetlands and utility corridor.

For this transaction, the buyer is underwriting and acquiring Phase One only — 88 lots. At $27,000 per planned Phase One lot, the asking price is $2,376,000. This is a residential land-basis acquisition opportunity for a developer or builder looking for meaningful scale in Horry County without having to acquire the entire larger Hucks Tract master concept.

Regional Growth Catalyst — Horry County Rural Civic Center

Horry County has just moved a major regional catalyst project closer to construction along this same U.S. Highway 501 corridor. The county has completed the technical design phase for a new 500-acre Rural Civic Center at the intersection of Highways 501 and 22 in Conway. The project was approved in September 2025, and the county is targeting selection of a construction manager as early as September 2026.

The facility will include an indoor arena, an equestrian and livestock barn, and outdoor fairgrounds built to host rodeos, concerts, livestock shows, and county fairs — and will also function as a hurricane response resource, including vehicle marshalling and a National Guard Readiness Center for the region.

Local business owners are already citing meaningful spillover demand tied to the project: hotels, restaurants, gas stations, and feed/tack suppliers all see increased traffic during comparable events.

For a Phase One buyer, this is a relevant regional demand driver — it sits on the same US-501 corridor connecting Conway and Aynor, adding public investment, event-driven tourism, and household demand momentum in proximity to the Hucks Tract, alongside the existing planning and diligence package already in the file.
