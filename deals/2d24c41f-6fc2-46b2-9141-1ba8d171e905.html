Overview

Off-market land opportunity: 88 residential lots representing Phase One of a larger, 170-lot planned community (the "Hucks Tract") in Horry County, SC, positioned directly on the US-501 corridor between Aynor and Conway. Diligence has already been performed on the file — this is not a blind land buy. A completed Phase I Environmental Site Assessment and roadway geotechnical report are both available, along with a rezoning submission package.

The parcel sits along the same US-501 corridor as Horry County's newly approved 500-acre Rural Civic Center project, which is moving toward construction and is expected to bring additional growth momentum to the surrounding area.
