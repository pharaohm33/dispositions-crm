Local Land Market Context

Current small-lot inventory in 34475 generally supports asking prices in the high teens and low $20Ks depending on exact location, utilities, zoning, access, flood conditions, and buildability. There are also lower-priced lots in the market, so buyers should not assume every 0.20-acre parcel automatically supports $20K+.

The strongest value case will depend on confirming:

  • Building envelope
  • Title condition

Once those items are confirmed cleanly, the property becomes significantly easier for a builder or retail land buyer to underwrite.
