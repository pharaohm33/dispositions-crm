The Pitch

This is a 2013 block build, two stories, 2,907 square feet, four bedrooms, three bathrooms, two-car garage, brand-new 2026 HVAC, and the seller reports it is mostly move-in ready. We are asking $275,990 on the underlying contract — that is $95 a square foot on a house that is thirteen years old.

This is the largest of the three homes at the lowest price by a margin that is not close.

How the ARV Pencils

Run it on price per foot and the picture gets simple. The comp set is trading between $172 and $198 a square foot. The reports put the average at $185 to $186 a foot. At 2,907 square feet, even $160 a foot puts this house at $465,000.

We are calling the ARV $450,000+, which is $155 a foot — below every comp on this list on a per-foot basis, and the number we are asking buyers to underwrite against.

One honest note on the $485,000 same-street comp: that house has a pool and the subject does not. The comp report adjusts for the pool and still lands the adjusted value at $456,995. Even after removing the pool adjustment, that comp prints above our stated ARV, and it is 453 square feet smaller than the subject.

The subject is the largest house in this comp set. Buyer to run their own comps and confirm their own ARV.
