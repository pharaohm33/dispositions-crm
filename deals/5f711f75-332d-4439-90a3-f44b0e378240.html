Overview

This is a 2013 block build, two stories, 2,907 square feet, four bedrooms, three bathrooms, two-car garage, with a brand-new 2026 HVAC system, and the seller reports it is mostly move-in ready. JNA Dynamic Holdings LLC is calling the ARV $450,000+, which works out to $155 per square foot — below every comp on this list on a per-foot basis, and the number we are asking buyers to underwrite against.

One honest note on the $485,000 same-street comp: that home has a pool and the subject does not. The comp report adjusts for the pool and still lands the adjusted value at $456,995. Even after removing the pool premium, that comp prints above the stated ARV here, despite being 453 square feet smaller than the subject.

The subject is the largest house in this comp set. Buyer is expected to run their own comps and confirm their own ARV.
