It is currently a one-bathroom house in a market where every comparable has two or more, so a second bathroom belongs in the renovation scope.

The Square Footage — And Why the ARV Survives It

The listing shows 1,432 square feet. Seminole County tax records show 1,952 square feet. Total area is 2,780 square feet. That is a 520-foot gap, and it should be settled independently on a walkthrough or through the Seminole County Property Appraiser.

Here is what makes this deal workable either way, at an ARV of $346,152:

  • If the house is 1,952 sqft, ARV runs $177 per foot — below the $222 comparable average and well below the $261 a foot that a nearby comparable cleared in September.
  • If the house is 1,432 sqft, ARV runs $242 per foot — inside the $245 to $285 range that smaller Sanford 3-bedroom homes have closed at this year.

Most deals lose their footing when square footage comes back light. This one does not. Verify it anyway — it changes the renovation budget and exit price even where it does not change whether the deal works.

How to Read This

Two independent per-foot reads bracket this property. The larger-home set averages $222 a foot. The smaller-home set averages $271 a foot. The best size match in either set, at 2,111 square feet and built in 1931, closed at $261 a foot after 70 days on market.

The $346,152 ARV sits under all three of those figures on the tax-record square footage — it is not a stretch number.

The discipline side: larger homes trade at a lower price per foot than smaller ones, so the $271 figure should not be scaled up. The 2015-construction comparable is a different product than this 1950 wood-frame house. The single bathroom needs to become two before this property comps to anything above current levels.

There is also a separate as-is comp set in the Goldsboro pocket west of this location, nearer W 12th Street, where 4-bedroom homes sold at $120,000 and $138,800 this year. That is a different submarket from Pinehurst 1st Addition, and it is why diligence on location matters as much as diligence on scope. Walk both pockets if unfamiliar with Sanford.

At 0.48 acres and 20,502 square feet, this parcel is substantially larger than a typical Sanford infill lot, and larger than every comparable in either set, where the average lot size is 0.30 acres. That is value none of the comparables capture, and none of it is reflected in the $346,152 ARV.
