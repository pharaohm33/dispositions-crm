The pricing story is straightforward. A neighboring lot is currently being marketed around $67,000. The subject is being offered at $54,900. That gives the buyer an immediate pricing advantage relative to visible neighboring inventory.

The surrounding community already contains established residential development, allowing a buyer to evaluate the property based on an existing neighborhood rather than undeveloped raw acreage.

Lockwood Folly has reportedly implemented restrictions affecting certain speculative home construction. Because community rules can change and may vary based on builder status, ownership structure, architectural approval, or intended use, buyer must independently confirm all current construction requirements before closing.

Buyer should specifically verify:

  • Spec-Home Restrictions
  • Owner-Builder Requirements
  • Approved Builder Requirements
  • Architectural Review
  • Minimum Home Size
  • Exterior Design Standards
  • Construction Timelines
  • HOA / POA Approval
  • Rental Restrictions, If Any
  • Setbacks
  • Any Additional Community Development Standards

No representation is made that unrestricted speculative construction is currently permitted.
