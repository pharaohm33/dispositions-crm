Overview

A rare ranch-estate assemblage in Parker County offered well below market — priced at $1,500,000 while carrying an estimated as-is value of $2,400,000. The offering is anchored by a spread of approximately 38 acres and includes two homes plus a fully stocked, approximately 7-acre bass lake.

The primary residence sits on a recorded lot of 3.81 acres and offers 3 bedrooms and 4,462 square feet of living space, with an attached garage for parking. The home was originally built in 1974.

Because this is being marketed off-market, we ask that interested parties treat the location as private — the address is disclosed only to serious, one-off buyers and is available upon request; it is not to be shared or posted publicly.

This is an assignment of contract. JNA Dynamic Holdings LLC holds an equitable interest under contract with the underlying property owner and is marketing that contract position for assignment — this is not a direct sale by, and does not imply direct contact with, the underlying seller/owner.
