Market Context — Parker County Ranch Land

Parker County ranches currently list at an average of approximately $2.9M, with an average of approximately $27,000 per acre and a county median around $30,400 per acre — mostly for raw or lightly improved acreage.
Working coastal Bermuda pasture in the county is marketed around $15,900 per acre before any improvements.
The subject pencils to approximately $39,400 per acre — and the premium over raw dirt buys two homes, a pool, three barns and shops, and a 7-acre private lake already standing.
Replicating this setup would mean buying the dirt at county median, then building two homes, digging and stocking a 7-acre lake, and erecting the barns — costs that would exceed $1.5M before the first hay cut.
The Poolville corridor is established ranch territory — large holdings including a 700-acre legacy ranch sit 2 miles from town, and improved ranches on Zion Hill Road trade in this same pocket.
At $1.5M, the subject sits in the most liquid tier of the Parker County ranch market — below the $2.4M average ranch sale, where DFW buyer demand is deepest.
