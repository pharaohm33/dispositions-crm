About This Opportunity

This is a 13-home single-family portfolio located in Rockford, Winnebago County, Illinois, offered at $1,476,923 against an after-repair value of $2,000,000, for a gross margin of $523,077. The portfolio is turnkey, with a combined estimated repair budget of up to $10,000.

Of the 13 homes, 10 are currently occupied and 3 are vacant. The portfolio generates a current monthly rent roll of $19,632, translating to an estimated NOI yield of approximately 8.46%, at roughly $123,077 per door. Seven of the units carry HAP-backed (housing assistance) rental income, adding to the stability of the in-place cash flow.

A representative unit within the portfolio is a single-family home built in 1958, with 4 bedrooms, 1 full bath, and 1 half-bath across 2,042 square feet on a 0.16-acre lot, with driveway parking. The property is in turn-key condition with a roof aged 1–3 years, a heating system aged 0–5 years, central air conditioning, and a full basement foundation in good condition; it is currently tenant occupied.

Assignment of Contract: This opportunity is being offered as an assignment of contract. JNA Dynamic Holdings LLC holds an equitable interest under a purchase agreement with the property owner and is marketing that contract position; this is not a direct sale by JNA Dynamic Holdings LLC, and interested parties will not be dealing directly with the underlying seller.
