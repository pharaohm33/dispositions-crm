The Opportunity

Two things make this deal, and we're putting both at the top.

First, the leases are NNN. Tenants carry taxes, insurance and maintenance. You are not the one writing the check when a compressor goes down or the tax bill lands. On a 9,100 square foot medical campus with an $18,869 annual tax bill, that structure is worth real money every year you own it.

Second, the seller will carry $1,225,000. Entry is $1,100,000. That puts a buyer into a Mesa medical office campus with roughly 47 percent down and no bank underwriting for eight weeks before declining.

The capital work is already done. Roof is three years old. All newer AC units. New asphalt across a 66-space lot. New paint, lighting and signage. Roof, HVAC and asphalt are the three line items that gut a commercial pro forma in the first five years, and every one of them has been addressed.
