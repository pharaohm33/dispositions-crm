How To Read This Honestly

  • Both same-street comps are renovated. The subject is maintained, not renovated — that gap is the scope of work and the core argument on this property.
  • Both comps carry boating infrastructure the subject does not have: a new seawall, 30-foot dock, and 27,000 lb lift on one; sailboat depth and two lifts on the other. The subject has a private dock and no lift — a real adjustment on this street.
  • Comp #2 listed at $3,295,000 and closed at $2,850,000. The street resisted pricing above $2.85M — treat $2.85M as evidence of a ceiling, not a floor.
  • Cutting the other way: the subject is larger than both comps at 4,200 sq ft versus 3,496 and 3,799 sq ft, and sits on a larger lot — roughly 17,900 sq ft versus 15,078 and 14,650 sq ft.
  • A third-party CMA on this property adjusts the two comp closes upward for size to $2,964,952 and $3,050,277, landing at a $3,007,614 midpoint. That figure is not being published as the ARV here — the adjustment is driven by square footage while the subject has fewer bedrooms than both comps. The ARV is being held at $2,800,000. The full CMA is available on request.
  • No projected profit figure is being published. The gap between $1,850,000 and the comp evidence above is visible — scope, carry, insurance, and exit timeline are for the buyer to model.

Exit Strategies

  • Renovate and resell — the primary play. Two renovated houses on this street cleared in 8 and 14 days in September, indicating a fast market for finished waterfront product priced right.
  • Add the boat lift — both comps that set the ceiling have lifts; the subject has a dock and no lift. This is a known, quotable scope item that moves the property toward the comps.
  • Hold — no HOA, so short-term and annual rental are open to the buyer subject to Sarasota County rules (buyer to confirm). No rent figure is published here, as no verified rent comps for 4,200 sq ft waterfront in this pocket are available.
