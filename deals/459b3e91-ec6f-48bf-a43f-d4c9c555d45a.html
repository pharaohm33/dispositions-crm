How To Read This Honestly

Three factors cut against a straight read of the two same-street closes:

  • Both comparables are renovated. The subject is maintained, not renovated — that gap is the scope of work and is central to this deal's thesis.
  • Both comparables carry boating infrastructure the subject does not have — a new seawall, 30-foot dock and 27,000 lb lift on one; sailboat depth and two lifts on the other. The subject has a private dock and no lift, which is a real adjustment on this street.
  • The $2,850,000 comparable was listed at $3,295,000 and closed at $2,850,000 — treat $2.85M as evidence of the ceiling, not the floor.

Cutting the other way: the subject is larger than both comparables at 4,200 sq ft against 3,496 and 3,799 sq ft, and sits on a larger lot — roughly 17,900 sq ft against 15,078 and 14,650 sq ft.

A third-party CMA on this property adjusts the two closes upward to $2,964,952 and $3,050,277 on the basis of that size difference, landing at a $3,007,614 midpoint. This is not being published as our ARV — the adjustment is driven by square footage while the subject has fewer bedrooms than both comparables. ARV remains published at $2,800,000. The full CMA is available on request. No projected profit figure is being published — scope, carry, insurance and exit timeline will determine the outcome of your own model.

Exit Strategies

  • Renovate and resell — the primary play. Two renovated homes on this street closed quickly in September, indicating a fast market for finished waterfront product priced right.
  • Add a boat lift — both comparables that set the pricing ceiling have lifts; the subject has a dock and no lift. This is a known, quotable scope item that moves the property toward the comparables.
  • Hold — no HOA means short-term and annual rental may be open subject to Sarasota County rules; confirm independently. No rent figure is being published, as there are no verified rent comparables for 4,200 sq ft waterfront in this pocket.
