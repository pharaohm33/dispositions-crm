How to Read This Honestly

Three things cut against a straight read of the two same-street closes, and we would rather you hear them from us:

  • Both comps are renovated. The subject is maintained, not renovated. That gap is your scope of work and it is the whole argument on this property.
  • Both comps carry boating infrastructure the subject does not have — a new seawall, 30-foot dock and 27,000 lb lift on one; sailboat depth and two lifts on the other. The subject has a private dock and no lift. On this street that is a real adjustment and you should make it.
  • Comp 2 listed at $3,295,000 and closed at $2,850,000. The street resisted above $2.85M. Treat $2.85M as evidence of the ceiling, not the floor.

Cutting the other way: the subject is larger than both comps at 4,200 sq ft against 3,496 and 3,799, and sits on a bigger lot — roughly 17,900 sq ft against 15,078 and 14,650.

A third-party CMA on this property adjusts those two closes upward to $2,964,952 and $3,050,277 on that size difference and lands at a $3,007,614 midpoint. We are not publishing that as our ARV. The adjustment is driven by square footage while the subject has fewer bedrooms than both comps, and we are not going to raise our ARV in the same week we cut our price. The full CMA is available on request — read it and decide for yourself. We are staying at $2,800,000.

We are not publishing a projected profit figure. The gap between $1,785,000 and the comp evidence is visible above. Your scope, your carry, your insurance, your exit timeline. Build your own model.

Exit Strategies
