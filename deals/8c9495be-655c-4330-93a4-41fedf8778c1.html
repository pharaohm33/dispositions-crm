Here Is What We Have

This is an off-market custom-home lot inside Coyote Pass, the gated estate section of Tucson's Starr Pass master-planned community.

The subject contains approximately 0.744 acres / 32,441 square feet, is classified as vacant residential land, and carries R-1 zoning.

This is not an isolated desert parcel. Coyote Pass is already built out with substantial custom residences, paved streets, gated access, and utility infrastructure. Comparable vacant lots within Coyote Pass report public water, sewer, electricity, and other utilities at or on the site, although the buyer must independently verify the exact stub locations and connection requirements for the subject lot.

The subject also benefits from an open rear orientation and desert-view setting, creating the opportunity for a custom residence designed around Tucson's mountain, sunset, and city-light environment.

And the finished-product support is directly next door: 3232 W Tumamoc Dr sold for $1,170,000 on November 24, 2025. That home contains approximately 3,433 SF on roughly 0.74 acres — essentially the same lot-size category as the subject.

At $165,000, this is a land-entry opportunity into a gated custom-home pocket where seven-figure finished product already exists on the same street.

Why This Lot Makes Sense

The biggest advantage here is the relationship between the land basis and finished-product environment.

Directly next door, 3232 W Tumamoc Dr closed at $1.17 million in November 2025. The home contains 4 bedrooms, 3.5 baths and approximately 3,433 square feet on roughly 0.74 acres.

That does not mean every home built on the subject will sell for $1.17 million. It does prove that buyers have already paid seven figures for custom residential product on this exact street and on a very similar lot size. Other surrounding properties also demonstrate the upper-end residential character of Tumamoc Drive, with existing custom homes throughout the Coyote Pass section.

For a custom builder, the play is straightforward:

  • Acquire the homesite
  • Design around the views and terrain
  • Verify the existing utility infrastructure
  • Engineer the site correctly
  • Build into an established luxury residential pocket instead of creating the market from scratch

Starr Pass Lifestyle

Coyote Pass sits inside the broader Starr Pass environment on Tucson's scenic west side.

The nearby JW Marriott Tucson Starr Pass Resort & Spa and The Club at Starr Pass offer access to a major resort and golf environment. The Club currently operates 27 holes of championship desert golf, including an Arnold Palmer-designed nine.

Starr Pass also sits directly alongside Tucson's mountain recreation network. The nearby Starr Pass trail system connects into Tucson Mountain Park, which offers hiking, trail running, mountain biking and other non-motorized trail recreation.
