This house needs everything — not paint and carpet, everything. There is a professional condition report on the property and it is not a pretty document. Roof over twenty years old and leaking, both HVAC systems over twenty years old, plumbing needing full replacement, an electrical panel with double-lugged breakers and cut wires, fungal growth in the crawlspace and through walls and ceilings, kitchen cabinets already removed, trash and animal waste inside, and three rear additions that appear to have been built without permits. If you are looking for a light flip, this is not it.

Here is why it's still worth a look: $155,000 on a 2,470 square foot house sitting on 16,988 square feet in 27104, one of the better addresses in Winston-Salem. That is $63 a square foot going in. Renovated product within a mile is closing between $182 and $190 a foot, with three sales this year at $430,000 and $455,000. Ugly house, good street, big spread, and a contractor's scope of work.
