Overview

Large office/flex asset with I-84 visibility and heavy existing infrastructure. Per the seller, both current tenants, FOX61 and the Hartford Courant, are staying, and the seller reports a letter of intent from a logistics company for additional space. The play is commercial lease-up and stabilization, with potential adaptive reuse subject to approvals.

Property Snapshot

  • Commercial office/flex building; built/expanded 1928, 1970, and 1987
  • Zoning: CX-1 and MX-2
  • Offices, studios, production space, multi-level warehouse, cafeteria, and conference space
  • Production-room ceiling height approximately 39 ft 8 in
  • Three passenger and two freight elevators; three independent power feeds
  • 423 parking spaces reported, including 186 City-leased spaces through March 31, 2028 at $40 per space per month ($89,280/year). Transferability to be verified.
  • I-84 frontage, with quick access to I-84/I-91, near Union Station
  • Land: approximately 4.39 acres
  • 2024 property tax: $326,658
  • Walk Score: 89; area schools reported as 3 below average
  • Tenant occupied
