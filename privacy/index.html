Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 11, 2026

Suede Buffalo LLC ("SendMyBuyer," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at sendmybuyer.com (the "Site"), a platform where we share real estate investment opportunities, including wholesale and off-market deals across the United States, with buyers, and where wholesalers, realtors, and other partners can work with our disposition team. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site you agree to this policy.

Text messaging (SMS) and mobile numbers. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as a messaging provider that delivers our texts, does not include mobile opt-in data or consent. See Section 6.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, optional mobile phone number, a password (stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form), and whether you are a buyer, wholesaler, realtor, or other.
  • Buy box and preferences: the states, cities, strategies, asset classes, financing types, and notes you tell us you are looking for, and for partners, the areas where you would like to work deals.
  • Payment-contact details (partners, optional): an entity name and a link to a document you control that tells us how to pay you. Please never enter bank account or routing numbers anywhere on the Site.
  • Text-message consent records: if you check the SMS consent box at sign-up, we record that you agreed, the date and time, the mobile number, the exact consent wording shown to you, and the page where you agreed.
  • Messages and activity you submit: deal interest, address-access requests, help-chat questions, and anything you send to us by email, phone, or text.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic account and usage information, such as when you last signed in and which deals you viewed or asked about.
  • A session token stored in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We do not use it to track you across other websites.

Information from other sources

  • Business contact information that is publicly available or that you have given to us or to our partners in connection with real estate transactions, such as property-ownership records for investors. We contact these people only as the law allows. For text messages, we send marketing texts only to people who have given us their consent as described in Section 6.

2. How we use information

  • To create and run your account and show you the deals you have access to.
  • To match deals to your buy box and tell you about opportunities that fit it.
  • To respond to questions, address-access requests, and support requests.
  • To send you messages by email and, only if you opted in, by text, as described in Section 6.
  • To keep the Site secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep records of consent.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site, and to meet legal obligations.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing. We share it only in these limited cases:

  • Service providers that help us run the Site and our messaging, such as Google (which hosts our Site data through Google Workspace and Apps Script), our email provider, and our text-messaging provider. They may use the information only to provide services to us. As explained in Section 6, this never includes sharing mobile numbers or text consent for marketing.
  • Transaction parties, when needed to carry out a real estate deal you are part of, for example sending your offer or proof-of-funds details to the seller side of a deal you asked about.
  • Legal and safety reasons, such as responding to a lawful request, enforcing our Terms, or protecting rights, property, or safety.
  • Business transfers, if we merge with, or sell part of our business to, another company. Any new owner must honor this policy for information already collected.

4. Your choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP to any text to opt out. See Section 6.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or email us.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: email info@sendmybuyer.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may keep limited records where the law requires it or where we need them to protect ourselves in a dispute, such as proof that a person opted out.
  • State privacy rights: depending on where you live (for example, California, Colorado, Virginia, and others), you may have the right to know, access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted ads. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

5. Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including hashed passwords and access limited to authorized team members. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We keep information for as long as your account is active and as needed for the purposes above, for legal and accounting reasons, and to keep consent and opt-out records.

6. Text messaging (SMS) privacy

  • We send text messages only to people who have agreed to receive them, for example by checking the unchecked-by-default SMS consent box when creating an account on the Site, or who text us first. Agreeing to texts is never required to create an account or to purchase anything.
  • We use your mobile number only to send the messages you agreed to receive and to answer you.
  • Your mobile number and your text-message opt-in or consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We share them only with the service providers that deliver our texts, and only so that they can deliver those texts for us.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for help. For full program details see our SMS Terms.

7. Children

The Site is for businesses and adults and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Links to other sites

The Site may link to other websites such as Google Maps, Zillow, or Redfin. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new "last updated" date. If a change materially affects how we use your mobile number, we will ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
